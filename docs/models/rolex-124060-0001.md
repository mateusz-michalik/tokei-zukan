# Rolex 124060-0001 "Submariner" — illustration notes

Drawn to the [watch illustration standard](../watch-illustration-standard.md), 2 November 2026, and refined the same day for depth, softer reflections, a recessed rehaut and the night rendering. The first Rolex in the catalogue.

## Reference and dimensions

- **Photo:** Rolex's front-on m124060-0001 product photo (800 × 1180 px, AVIF with a transparent background) and its night-mode image, supplied for this work. Neither is stored in the repository.
- **Specs:** [m124060-0001 on Rolex](https://www.rolex.com/watches/submariner/m124060-0001), checked 2 November 2026:
  - **Case:** Oyster, 41 mm, Oystersteel; monobloc middle case, screw-down case back and winding crown.
  - **Bezel:** unidirectional rotatable 60-minute graduated, scratch-resistant Cerachrom insert in black ceramic, numerals and graduations coated in platinum.
  - **Crown:** screw-down, Triplock triple waterproofness system.
  - **Crystal:** scratch-resistant sapphire. No date, so no Cyclops lens.
  - **Water resistance:** 300 metres / 1,000 feet.
  - **Movement:** calibre 3230, Manufacture Rolex; perpetual, mechanical, self-winding; −2/+2 s/day after casing; about 70 hours. Rolex's page data gives a frequency of 28,800 vph, as SJX, aBlogtoWatch and Monochrome report ("28,800 beats per hour (4 Hz)"), so `beats` is 8.
  - **Bracelet:** Oyster, three-piece solid links; folding Oysterlock safety clasp with Glidelock extension.
- **Variant:** black dial, black Cerachrom bezel, Oyster bracelet: the 2020 no-date Submariner. Not the 126610LN Submariner Date and not its date geometry or Cyclops.
- **Dimensions Rolex doesn't publish:**
  - **Lug width:** 21 mm. Rolex's own page data gives the bracelet's width as 21 (bracelet 97JB00); [Monochrome](https://monochrome-watches.com/rolex-submariner-124060-41mm-no-date-2020-review-price/): "increased from 20mm in the past to 21mm". The photo agrees: the end link is 195 px, 21.0 mm at the photo's scale.
  - **Lug to lug:** 47.6 mm, from [Monochrome](https://monochrome-watches.com/rolex-submariner-history-part-4-modern-references/) ("Lug-to-lug measurement is also identical, at 47.6mm") and [Fratello](https://www.fratellowatches.com/sunday-morning-showdown-tudor-black-bay-master-chronometer-vs-rolex-submariner-124060/) ("a 47.6mm lug-to-lug").
  - **Thickness:** 12.5 mm, the most quoted figure, from [Monochrome's 2020 review](https://monochrome-watches.com/rolex-submariner-124060-41mm-no-date-2020-review-price/) ("around 12.5mm"). It's approximate: Monochrome's 2025 history gives "12mm" and Fratello "13mm", SJX gives "Unavailable", and no caliper measurement was found.
  - **Clasp width:** not verified, so the bracelet uses the shared oyster taper (3 mm narrower at the clasp).
- **Scale:** one uniform 9.28 px/mm, from the bezel (≈380 px across for 41 mm) and checked against the end link (195 px for 21 mm). At that scale the photo's case is 48.55 mm lug to lug against 47.6, so the case maps photo millimetres onto the specs with factors of 1 across and 0.98 along.
- **Perspective:** the photo is taken slightly from below and to the left. The lug tips' midpoint sits 1.1 mm below the hand pivot, and the bezel's raised edge appears about 0.4 mm left of the pivot. The drawing centres all three: the case was measured about the lug midpoint, the dial about the pivot and the bezel about its own centre. The lower dial markers sit about 0.3 mm further out in the photo than the upper ones for the same reason; the drawing keeps them symmetric.

## Where it lives

- **Selection:** `shape: "rolex-1240600001"` sends the drawing to `rolex1240600001SVG()` in [rolex1240600001.ts](../../src/render/rolex1240600001.ts).
- **Bracelet:** the strap renderer's `oyster` style with this watch's proportions, passed from [watch.ts](../../src/render/watch.ts): centre links 0.43 of the width and a long 8.8 mm link pitch (`linkPitch`; every other oyster keeps 5.2 mm). All brushed, centre links staggered half a link, as in the photo. `linkShade: 'flat'` shades the links as flat faces lit from above, as the photo shows them: a dark shadow at each 0.34 mm joint, a narrow lip, a bright upper face falling into shade where the link turns away, nearly square corners, and edges that roll off at the bracelet's sides. Other bracelets keep the shared rounded shading.
- **Bezel figures:** drawn strokes from [strokeDigits.ts](../../src/render/strokeDigits.ts) with the Submariner's own squared glyphs (`BEZEL_DIGITS`), not a font, so they look the same in every browser and in the share images.

## Live time

The hour, minute and seconds hands are tagged. The seconds hand sweeps at 8 beats a second (28,800 vph).

## Lume

Rolex documents it: "Highly legible Chromalight display with long-lasting blue luminescence", "large luminescent hour markers", and "a luminescent capsule on the zero marker". [Monochrome](https://monochrome-watches.com/rolex-submariner-124060-41mm-no-date-2020-review-price/) adds the hands ("hour markers and hands in 18k white gold, filled with blue glow Chromalight") and the seconds hand's "luminous dot". Rolex's own night image of this reference shows exactly those parts glowing: the markers (indices), the hour hand's Mercedes segments and tip and the minute hand's inlay (hands), the seconds dot (seconds) and the zero pip (bezel). Its glow reads as a deep turquoise, rgb(69, 155, 166) at hue 187°, in crisply defined shapes on near-black metal. The catalogue follows it: glow `#50D2E0` (that hue, saturated, without washed-out centres), `halo: 0.6` for a tighter, fainter halo, and `ambient: 0.5` so the case and bracelet keep half the usual light at Night. Dusk is unchanged. Certainty `documented`.

## Validation

- **Overlay:** the drawing was rendered at the photo's 9.28 px/mm and overlaid at 50%, aligned on the hand pivot. The bezel figures, bars and ticks, zero triangle and pip, markers, minute track, printing, hands, lug flanks, guards and crown line up. The overlays moved the bezel's minute ticks to the inner part of the insert, widened the zero triangle, replaced font numerals with the measured stroke figures, and reshaped the Mercedes hand and the seconds hand's tail.
- **Other views:** checked in the app in Details, Compare (side by side and outlines), On the wrist, Day, Dusk and Night, and with live time on (the hands followed the clock and the seconds swept in eighths). Also on every bracelet, NATO, rubber, leather and nylon sample, as a thumbnail and in the share image.
- **Budget:** at `strapLen: 12`, 249 elements and about 39.9 KB, within the 320-element and 50 KB limits (189 elements and 31 KB before the refinement; the engraving's three layers account for most of the rise).
- **Refinement:** checked at Details size and as thumbnails, with enlargements only to inspect details, against the product photo and Rolex's night image. Hand positions and marker geometry are unchanged.
- **No regressions:** every other catalogue drawing renders byte-for-byte as before (watch, outline, Dusk, Night, wrist and all sample straps), including after the shared bracelet and night changes.
- **Checks:** `npm run build` passes type checking, all tests and the production build.

## Remaining approximations

- **Type:** ROLEX and OYSTER PERPETUAL use Times New Roman (Tinos in the share images), not Rolex's lettering; the coronet is a simple five-prong drawing.
- **Insert:** the Cerachrom's reflections are two sectors fading along their arcs; the photo's are brighter, with curved edges.
- **Case:** the lug shading is a few gradients and bands; the case's curvature towards the lugs and the guards' facets aren't modelled.
- **Bracelet:** links at one pitch on the shared taper, with the shared brushing; Rolex doesn't state the clasp width or the end link's exact shape.
- **Thickness:** 12.5 mm is a press figure, not Rolex's; sources range from 12 to 13 mm.
