# Omega 310.30.42.50.01.001 "Speedmaster Moonwatch Professional" — illustration notes

Drawn to the [watch illustration standard](../watch-illustration-standard.md), 1 November 2026. The first Omega in the catalogue.

## Reference and dimensions

- **Photo:** Omega's front-on 310.30.42.50.01.001 product photo (1100 px square, PNG), supplied for this work. It is not stored in the repository.
- **Specs:** [310.30.42.50.01.001 on Omega Australia](https://www.omegawatches.com/en-au/watch-omega-speedmaster-moonwatch-professional-co-axial-master-chronometer-chronograph-42-mm-31030425001001), checked 1 November 2026:
  - **Case:** 42 mm, 13.54 mm thick, 47.50 mm lug to lug, 20 mm between the lugs, steel.
  - **Crystal:** Hesalite, with the Omega logo at its centre.
  - **Movement:** Omega 3861, manual winding, 50-hour reserve, 3 Hz.
  - **Water resistance:** 5 bar (50 m).
  - **Bracelet:** steel, "brushed five-arched-links-per-row", foldover clasp.
- **Variant:** black step dial, Hesalite crystal, steel bracelet. Not the sapphire-sandwich .002, the strap versions or any special edition.
- **Movement rate:** 3 Hz is 21,600 vph, so `beats` is 6. The dial's 1/3-second track matches it ([SJX](https://watchesbysjx.com/2021/01/05): "a correctly-graduated track with seconds hashmarks for 1/3th of a second").
- **Scale:** one uniform 13.5 px/mm. At that scale the photo's case is 42.25 mm across and 47.9 mm lug to lug, against Omega's 42 and 47.5, so the drawing maps photo millimetres onto the specs with factors of 0.994 and 0.992.
- **Perspective:** the photo is taken slightly from below the watch's face. The dial centre sits 1.0 mm above the midpoint of the lug tips, and the bezel 1.4 mm above it, because they stand higher than the lugs. The drawing centres all of them; the case was measured about the lug midpoint, the dial about the hand pivot, and the bezel about its own centre, 0.2 mm lower than the photo shows it.

## Where it lives

- **Selection:** `shape: "omega-31030425001001"` sends the drawing to `omega31030425001001SVG()` in [omega31030425001001.ts](../../src/render/omega31030425001001.ts).
- **Bracelet:** a `jubilee`-style bracelet drawn by the strap renderer's five-row `moonwatch` layout ([strap.ts](../../src/render/strap.ts)): wide arched outer and centre links with two narrow polished rows between them, deeper gaps and rounder corners than the SSK003's flat links, tapering to Omega's 15 mm clasp over 55 mm, and an end link divided the same way. The SSK003's five-row bracelet is the same mechanism with its own rows.
- **Figures:** the tachymeter figures are drawn strokes from [strokeDigits.ts](../../src/render/strokeDigits.ts), the Hamilton's numerals narrowed to 82% width. The register figures use the same helper with the Speedmaster's own squared glyphs (`SQUARE`). Both have Omega's plain-bar 1.

## Live time

The hour and minute hands and the running-seconds register are tagged; the register moves at 6 beats a second. The 30-minute and 12-hour counters and the central chronograph hand are tagged `chrono` and rest at zero.

## Lume

Omega's page doesn't name the luminous material or the lumed parts. The photo shows the same pale green-white paint on the batons and the two 12 o'clock dots, the stripes of the hour and minute hands and the chronograph hand's lozenge. Those are tagged as indices, hands and seconds (the chronograph seconds hand). The register hands and all printing are plain white and stay dark. Dealer listings describe Super-LumiNova; no night photo of this reference with a clear colour was found, so the glow is a generic green (`#8CFF6E`) with certainty `uncertain`.

## Validation

First drawn 1 November 2026; a review the same day corrected the pushers' direction (they had been tilted towards the crown), replaced the narrowed register figures with squared ones, softened the registers' rings, gave the lugs their twisted facets, arched and tapered the bracelet, and lightened TACHYMÈTRE and PROFESSIONAL.


- **Overlay:** the drawing was rendered at the photo's 13.5 px/mm and overlaid at 50%, aligned on the hand pivot for the dial and on the lug midpoint for the case. Batons, track, registers and their figures, hands, logo, lug tips, creases and flanks line up; the crown recess, guards and pushers were reshaped against it.
- **Other views:** checked in the app in Day, Dusk and Night, on the wrist, as an outline and a thumbnail, on the bracelet, leather, NATO and tropic rubber, and with live time on.
- **Budget:** at `strapLen: 12`, 241 elements and about 45.5 KB, within the 320-element and 50 KB limits, close to the other chronographs (Oris 239, SSC947 293).
- **Checks:** `npm run build` passes type checking, all tests and the production build.

## Remaining approximations

- **Figures:** stroke digits approximate Omega's tachymeter typeface; TACHYMÈTRE, OMEGA and PROFESSIONAL use Futura where the device has it, otherwise Century Gothic, Avenir Next or Arial. Speedmaster is a system script, not Omega's logotype.
- **Ω:** a simple stroked Ω for the applied logo and the one etched in the crystal.
- **Case:** flat tones for the brushed tops, sloped facets and polished bevels; the guards' facets and the case's curvature towards the lugs aren't modelled.
- **Bracelet:** the arched links are a gradient on flat shapes at one pitch (5 mm), and the 55 mm taper to the clasp is a stand-in: Omega doesn't give the bracelet's length.
- **Crystal:** the Hesalite's dome and its reflections are reduced to one faint highlight and a glint.
