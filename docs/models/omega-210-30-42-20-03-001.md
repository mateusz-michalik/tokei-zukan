# Omega 210.30.42.20.03.001 "Seamaster Diver 300M" — illustration notes

Drawn to the [watch illustration standard](../watch-illustration-standard.md), 10 November 2026. The second Omega in the catalogue, after the [Speedmaster](omega-310-30-42-50-01-001.md).

## Reference and dimensions

- **Photo:** Omega's front-on 210.30.42.20.03.001 product photo (1100 px square), supplied for this work, with the caseback, wrist and lifestyle photos from the same product page. None is stored in the repository.
- **Specs:** [210.30.42.20.03.001 on Omega Australia](https://www.omegawatches.com/en-au/watch-omega-seamaster-diver-300m-co-axial-master-chronometer-42-mm-21030422003001), checked 10 November 2026:
  - **Case:** 42 mm, 13.56 mm thick, 49.90 mm lug to lug, 20 mm between the lugs, steel.
  - **Crystal:** "Domed scratch-resistant sapphire crystal with anti-reflective treatment on both sides".
  - **Movement:** Omega 8800, self-winding, 55-hour reserve, 3.5 Hz.
  - **Water resistance:** 30 bar (300 m / 1000 ft).
  - **Bracelet:** steel, "Foldover clasp with comfort setting and diver extension".
  - **Description:** "a blue ceramic bezel with a white enamel diving scale. The dial is also polished blue ceramic and features laser-engraved waves and a date window at 6 o'clock. The skeleton hands and raised indexes are rhodium-plated and are filled with white Super-LumiNova, while the helium escape valve has been given a conical design."
- **Variant:** blue dial and bezel, steel on steel. Not the rubber-strap 210.32.42.20.03.001, the 210.30.42.20.03.003 with Summer Blue enamel, or any special edition.
- **Movement rate:** 3.5 Hz is 25,200 vph, so `beats` is 7, the first watch with that rate in the catalogue.
- **Scale:** one uniform 13.75 px/mm, from the bracelet's 20 mm at the case. At that scale the photo's case is 42.32 mm across at 9 and 49.6 mm lug to lug (inner tip corners), against Omega's 42 and 49.9, so the drawing maps photo millimetres onto the specs with factors of 0.992 and 1.006.
- **Perspective:** the photo is taken slightly from below. The hand pivot sits 0.25 mm above the lug tips' midpoint and the bezel's centre 0.25 mm above that. The case was measured about the lug midpoint, the dial about the pivot (radii checked at 12, 3, 6 and 9) and the bezel about its own centre; the drawing centres all three.

## Where it lives

- **Selection:** `shape: "omega-21030422003001"` sends the drawing to `omega21030422003001SVG()` in [omega21030422003001.ts](../../src/render/omega21030422003001.ts).
- **Bracelet:** a `jubilee`-style bracelet drawn by the strap renderer's nine-row `diver300m` layout ([strap.ts](../../src/render/strap.ts)), with an end link divided the same way. It shares the Speedmaster's arched links.
- **Figures:** the bezel figures are drawn strokes from [strokeDigits.ts](../../src/render/strokeDigits.ts), with the Diver's own wide squared glyphs (`BEZEL_DIGITS`).
- **Lume:** the minute hand is tagged `minute`, a lume part added for it, so it can glow green while the hour hand and markers glow blue.

## Live time

The hour, minute and seconds hands are tagged; the seconds hand moves at 7 beats a second, for the 8800's 25,200 vph.

## Lume

Omega's page says the skeleton hands and raised indexes are "filled with white Super-LumiNova". It doesn't name the bezel pip, the seconds hand or the glow colours.

[PrestigeTime's review of the 2018 Diver 300M](https://www.prestigetime.com/blog/2018-omega-seamaster-diver-300m-review.html) covers this reference and the black 210.30.42.20.01.001. It says the two glow differently: "The blue luminescent material is used for the index and dot hour markers, and the tip of the hour hand", and "a second green luminescent color is used for the bezel dot and the tip of the minute hand". [Time+Tide's review of 210.30.42.20.03.001](https://timeandtidewatches.com/omega-seamaster-professional-300m-diver-review/) describes the hands' "arrangement of luminous triangles and circles".

So the following are lit:

- **Blue:** the markers (`indices`), the hour hand's circle (`hands`) and the seconds hand's lollipop (`seconds`).
- **Green:** the minute hand's arrow (`minute`) and the bezel pip (`bezel`), set with `partGlow`.

The white strips along the hands' arms are drawn as polished metal, not lume, since the reviews name only the circles and triangles. The lollipop's lume is shown by its white paint and "circles" plural; no source names its colour, so it takes the hour hand's blue.

The glows are the standard blue (`#8FD8FF`) and green (`#8CFF6E`) grade colours, not measured from a night photo, so certainty is `likely`.

## Validation

- **Overlay:** the drawing was rendered at the photo's 13.75 px/mm and overlaid at 50%, aligned on the lug midpoint. The case outline, lug tips, swelling at 3, crown, valve, bezel figures, markers, date, printing and hands line up. The remaining doubling on the dial and bezel is the photo's 0.25 and 0.5 mm offsets, which the drawing centres.
- **Comparison:** at 7 and 1.2 px/mm beside the Speedmaster, Submariner, Black Bay 58 and PRX. The bracelet's joints were deepened and its links arched after a first pass read as stripes.
- **App:** checked in Details in Day and Night, on the wrist, in Compare side by side and as stacked outlines, in the owned box, and with live time on. Also on leather, NATO, a plain oyster and rubber at the 20 mm attachment. Prices were checked in all five currencies.
- **Budget:** at `strapLen: 12`, 277 elements and about 35.9 KB (6.5 KB gzipped), within the 320-element and 50 KB limits.
- **Regressions:** every other watch's seven views are byte-identical to before.
- **Refinement, same day:** after review against the photo:
  - **Lugs:** softer, broader polished highlights on the lugs, with a feathered, curved crease.
  - **Bracelet:** finer lines between rows, clearer breaks between links, and the softer satin face instead of arched links.
  - **Bezel and dial:** fuller, rounder bezel figures, stronger waves, and a distinct metallic rim on the pip.
  - **Crown:** moved in front of the case, out of a recess between the guards, as a rounded knurled crown rather than a ribbed strip with a pointed cap.
- **Checks:** `npm run build` passes type checking, all tests and the production build.

## Remaining approximations

- **Type:** OMEGA, PROFESSIONAL and the lower print use Futura where the device has it, otherwise Century Gothic, Avenir Next or Arial. Seamaster is a system brush script (Brush Script MT, Segoe Script or Snell Roundhand), thickened with a stroke; it isn't Omega's logotype. The date uses Helvetica Neue or Arial.
- **Ω:** a simple stroked Ω.
- **Waves:** one sine pattern; the photo's waves vary slightly in depth and spacing across the dial, and the laser engraving's relief is a pale crest over a fine shadow.
- **Bezel edge:** the scallops are shallow arcs between the hour corners, as the photo's polished band suggests; the edge's facets and grip aren't modelled.
- **Hands:** flat two-tone frames; the polished bevels along the arms are a single light line.
- **Bracelet:** flat shapes with the shared satin shading. The polished rows use the shared polished face, so they're less bright than in the photo. Omega doesn't give the clasp width, so the bracelet tapers as the shared default does.
- **Crystal:** the domed sapphire is reduced to one faint highlight.
