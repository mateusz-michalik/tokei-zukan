# Tissot T137.407.11.041.00 "PRX Powermatic 80 40mm" — illustration notes

Drawn to the [watch illustration standard](../watch-illustration-standard.md), 6 November 2026. The first Tissot in the catalogue.

## Reference and dimensions

- **Reference:** T137.407.11.041.00 (Tissot SKU T1374071104100), the 40 mm automatic with the blue waffle dial on its integrated steel bracelet. Not the blue quartz PRX (T137.410.11.041.00), the 35 mm automatic (T137.207.11.041.00) or the other 40 mm dial colours.
- **Photos:** Tissot's product images for this reference on its [Australian product page](https://www.tissotwatches.com/en-au/T1374071104100.html): the front image the owner supplied (1,680 px square), the caseback, the bracelet T605046447 laid flat, and the wrist shot. They aren't stored in the repository.
- **Specs:** [T1374071104100 on Tissot Australia](https://www.tissotwatches.com/en-au/T1374071104100.html), checked 6 November 2026:
  - **Case:** 40.00 mm wide, 39.50 mm "case length", 10.93 mm thick, 316L steel, see-through caseback. **Lug width:** 12.00 mm.
  - **Crystal:** sapphire with one anti-reflective coating on the bottom side.
  - **Movement:** Swiss automatic, 80-hour power reserve, Nivachron balance spring. The [2021 technical sheet](https://www.swatchgroup.com/sites/default/files/media-files/tissot-prx-40-205-powermatic-80_technical.en_.pdf) names the ETA calibre 80.111 beating at 3 Hz, 21,600 vph, so `beats` is 6.
  - **Water resistance:** 10 bar (100 m). **Weight:** 138 g.
  - **Bracelet:** T605046447, stainless steel, butterfly clasp with push buttons, interchangeable quick release.
- **Thickness:** the current page gives 10.93 mm; the 2021 technical sheet gives 11.3 mm. The catalogue uses Tissot's current figure for this exact reference. The 2021 sheet also gives a mineral glass back; the current page says only "see-through caseback", so the catalogue doesn't name its material.
- **Lug to lug:** 44 mm, horn tip to horn tip, from [Worn & Wound's hands-on](https://wornandwound.com/review/hands-on-with-the-funky-tissot-prx-powermatic-80/), which separates the top plane (39.5 mm, Tissot's "case length"), the case's tips (44 mm) and the first articulating bracelet joint (nearly 51 mm). Tissot's caseback image corroborates it: 44.5 mm tip to tip at the scale where the case is 40 mm across, with a 12.2 mm opening between the horns. The front photo shows 43.3 mm, because the horns slope down away from the camera.
- **12 mm lug width:** the opening between the horns at 12 and 6, where the bracelet's first link enters (12.0 mm on the front photo). The bracelet itself is 27.3 mm across at the horn tips and continues the flanks' line.
- **Scale:** one uniform 19.675 px/mm, at which the case is 40 mm across at 3 and 9 (Tissot's 1,680 px front image). The dial centre is at (836.5, 840).

## Where it lives

- **Selection:** `shape: "t1374071104100"` sends the drawing to `t1374071104100SVG()` in [t1374071104100.ts](../../src/render/t1374071104100.ts).
- **Plan:** x scales with the case width; y follows the photo up to the crease where the top plane meets the horns (19.31 mm), and the horns' slopes are stretched to the catalogue's 22 mm half lug to lug.
- **Bracelet:** the strap renderer's `oyster` style at its `fitted` 27.3 mm, with the new `stepped` links, measured on Tissot's flat bracelet image (scaled by the links' 11.1 mm middles, as on the front photo):
  - **Links:** single pieces across the whole width. Each link's middle, 11.1 mm wide, stands 1.5 mm proud of its outer parts towards the case, so the joints step round the next link's middle. The launch sheet calls it a "single link" bracelet "based on the series of large links that take up its whole width".
  - **Pitch:** 6.3 mm, from the flat image. The front photo shows about 3 mm, because the bracelet turns away from the camera there; that view isn't used for the pitch.
  - **Finish:** brushed along the bracelet (`brush: "along"`), on the shared `slab` face, with a polished `bevel` 0.75 mm deep along each edge facing the case, and 0.3 mm joints.
  - **First link:** its middle is the full 12 mm wide and runs into the case's opening, to 19.95 mm from the centre, leaving a dark gap above the opening's floor as the photo shows. There's no separate end link.
  - **Taper:** from 27.3 mm at the horn tips to 17.2 mm, 39 mm out (`clasp`, `taperLen`). The flat image gives 17.2 mm where it stops narrowing; the front photo's widths at successive joints (27.1, 24.7, 23.1, 21.5, 20.1 mm) give the rate. Tissot doesn't publish either width.
  - **Other bracelets:** the stepped links and taper apply only to this fitted bracelet. A plain bracelet, NATO, rubber or leather strap is drawn at the 12 mm attachment, in the opening.

## Live time

The hour, minute and seconds hands are tagged. The seconds hand sweeps at 6 beats a second (21,600 vph). Watched running in the app: at 8:35:19 the hands stood at 257.7°, 212° and 114°, and the seconds hand stepped on.

## Lume

Tissot's sources disagree on the indices:
- **Hands only:** the current product page ("Luminescent material on the hands: Super-LumiNova®") and the German 2021 fact sheet ("Stabförmige Stunden- und Minutenzeiger mit SuperLumiNova®-Beschichtung", with "Applizierte Stundenindexe").
- **Hands and indices:** the English 2021 technical sheet ("Hour and minute baton hands coated with Super-LumiNova®", "Appliqué indexes coated with Super-LumiNova®"), and retailer listings.

The product photo shows the batons' inlays in the same white as the hands' inlays, so both are lit (`hands`, `indices`). The grey channels, the seconds hand, the date and the printing aren't. No night photo with a clear colour or a grade was found, so the glow is a generic green (`#8CFF6E`) with certainty `uncertain`.

## Remaining approximations

- **Lettering:** standard fonts, not Tissot's artwork; TISSOT's wide letterforms and 1853's humanist figures are approximated by fitted lengths.
- **Date:** shows a fixed 8.
- **Case:** drawn flat. The top plane's facets are suggested by the crease and the horns' shading, not modelled.
- **Bracelet:** the links' sides are drawn straight along the taper; on the photo each link is slightly wider at its case end, so the edge steps a little at each joint.
- **Clasp and taper:** the clasp width and where the taper ends come from Tissot's flat bracelet image, not a published figure.
- **Reflections:** the flange's mirror images of the markers and SWISS MADE aren't drawn.

## Validation

- **Overlay:** rendered at the photo's 19.675 px/mm and overlaid at 50% on the dial centre. The case outline, horn tips and opening, crown, bezel, flange, dial edge, markers, minute dashes, date frame, hands and printing line up. The bracelet's links are longer than the photo's, because the photo is foreshortened as the bracelet turns away (see Bracelet).
- **Other views:** checked in Details in Day, Dusk and Night and in all five currencies; on every strap type (as sold, bracelet, NATO, rubber and leather); in Compare side by side and as stacked outlines with the Tsuyosa and the Casios; on a 17 cm wrist; as a thumbnail; in the owned watch box; and as the share image.
- **Budget:** at `strapLen: 12`, 206 elements and about 20.3 KB (4.0 KB gzipped), within the 320-element and 50 KB limits. The waffle is one pattern rather than about 500 squares.
- **No regressions:** every other catalogue drawing renders byte-for-byte as on `main` in all seven views, apart from the night pass's filter numbering, which counts every drawing rendered.
- **Checks:** `npm run build` passes type checking, all tests and the production build.
