- **Unit:** `k`, the outer radius of the black aluminium bezel insert. With the 120-tooth coin-edge rim reaching `1.036 k` at the 40.0 mm case diameter (`R = 20.0 mm`), `k = R / 1.036 ≈ 19.305 mm`.
