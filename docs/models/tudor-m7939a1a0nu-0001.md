# Tudor M7939A1A0NU-0001 "Black Bay 58" — illustration notes

Drawn to the [watch illustration standard](../watch-illustration-standard.md), 2 November 2026, with the techniques refined on the [Rolex Submariner](rolex-124060-0001.md). The first Tudor in the catalogue.

## Reference and dimensions

- **Generation:** the current Black Bay 58. The generation began with the burgundy M7939A1A0RU in April 2025 (new case, calibre MT5400-U, five-link bracelet and T-fit clasp); this black-and-gilt reference followed at Watches & Wonders on 14 April 2026. It replaces the original M79030N-0001 the project card was first planned around: the original has been replaced on Tudor's site, and this is the model visitors can buy.
- **Photo:** Tudor's front-on M7939A1A0NU-0001 product photo (900 × 1200 px, AVIF with a transparent background and a soft drop shadow to the right), supplied for this work. It isn't stored in the repository. The shadow and Tudor's logo overlap the lower right, so measurements come from the left side.
- **Specs:** [M7939A1A0NU-0001 on Tudor](https://www.tudorwatch.com/en/watches/black-bay-58/m7939a1a0nu-0001) and Tudor's [Watches & Wonders 2026 press kit](https://media.tudorwatch.com/image/upload/fl_attachment/v1/tudorwatch/09d6x9svgwnds/pressroom/black-bay-58/pdf/NP2026_Black_Bay_58-en.pdf), checked 2 November 2026:
  - **Case:** 39 mm steel, polished and satin; 11.7 mm thick and 47.8 mm lug to lug (press kit: "39mm diameter, 11.7mm thickness, 47.8mm lug to lug"). Screw-down crown with the Tudor rose.
  - **Bezel:** unidirectional, steel with an anodised aluminium insert, 60-minute scale, red triangle with a lumed pearl.
  - **Crystal:** domed sapphire. **Dial:** matt black, domed, applied hour markers.
  - **Water resistance:** 200 m.
  - **Movement:** Manufacture Calibre MT5400-U, self-winding, COSC and METAS (Master Chronometer), 28,800 vph, 65 hours, so `beats` is 8.
  - **Bracelet:** five-link steel, polished and satin, T-fit clasp.
- **Lug width:** 20 mm. Tudor doesn't publish it; Monochrome gives 20 mm for the burgundy reference, which has the same case. The photo agrees: the lug openings measure 19.6 mm at the photo's scale, the same 2% under as the lug-to-lug below.
- **Not published:** the bracelet's taper and clasp width, so it uses the shared taper.
- **Scale:** one uniform 14.05 px/mm, at which the bezel is 39 mm across.
- **Perspective:** the photo's case is only 45.6 mm lug to lug against Tudor's 47.8: the lugs turn down away from the camera, so they read short. The drawing keeps Tudor's dimension, as the standard asks: the case outline is drawn directly at 39 × 47.8 mm, following the photo's plan (broad shoulders, full flanks) rather than stretching the photo's lugs. The lug tips' midpoint sits 0.4 mm below the hand pivot; the case was measured about the lug midpoint and the dial and bezel about the pivot.

## Where it lives

- **Selection:** `shape: "m7939a1a0nu0001"` sends the drawing to `m7939a1a0nu0001SVG()` in [m7939a1a0nu0001.ts](../../src/render/m7939a1a0nu0001.ts).
- **Bracelet:** a `jubilee`-style bracelet drawn by the strap renderer's five-row `bb58` layout ([strap.ts](../../src/render/strap.ts)), measured on the photo:
  - **Rows:** wide satin outer links (0.272 of the width each) and three polished centre rows (0.15 each). The middle row breaks with the outer links; the rows either side are offset half a link. Pitch 5.6 mm (5.0 mm on the photo's upper half, 6.3 on the lower, where the bracelet curves differently).
  - **Taper:** from 20 mm at the lugs towards 16 mm 30 mm out, as the photo shows; Tudor doesn't publish the clasp width.
  - **Per-row finishes**, new in the five-row table (`rowFinish`, `rowGap`):
    - **Outer rows** (`satin`): light, with rounded corners and fine lengthwise brushing, lit across the upper half and shading softly into each joint.
    - **Rows either side of the middle** (`flat`): mirror links divided by thin 0.14 mm lines, each with a bright lip under the line and a face brightening downwards.
    - **Middle row** (`pill`): separate rounded links whose ends roll away into deep 0.7 mm shadowed gaps.
  - **End link** (`through`): the polished rows run on through it to the case, so it has pieces only for the outer rows, and the brushing lies only on the brushed rows. The joints are near-black.
- **Bezel figures:** drawn strokes from [strokeDigits.ts](../../src/render/strokeDigits.ts) with the BB58's own rounded-square glyphs (`BEZEL_DIGITS`).

## Live time

The hour, minute and seconds hands are tagged. The seconds hand sweeps at 8 beats a second (28,800 vph).

## Lume

Tudor's press kit names "Grade A Swiss Super-LumiNova" for the Snowflake hands; Fratello and Monochrome describe the lumed markers, and Tudor the lumed pearl on the bezel. The seconds hand's dot is the same cream as the markers in the product photo, so it's counted as lume, though no source confirms it. By day the lume is beige ("filled with luminescent material in beige", Monochrome). No night photo or glow colour was found for this generation, so the glow is a generic green (`#8CFF6E`), as beige Super-LumiNova usually glows, with certainty `uncertain`.

## Validation

- **Overlay:** the drawing was rendered at the photo's 14.05 px/mm and overlaid at 50%, aligned on the hand pivot. The markers, hands, minute track, printing, bezel graduations and figures, zero triangle and pearl, crown and lug flanks line up; the lugs run 5% longer than the photo, as explained above.
- **Reviews:** two reviews refined the drawing. The first compared the bracelet link by link, which gave the per-row finishes, the polished rows running through the end link and the near-black joints. The second redrew the lugs as broad shoulders with fitted end links, a narrow bevel and broad brushed tops; tapered the bracelet and lightened and rounded its outer links; enlarged the red triangle and pearl; lightened the crystal edge; and rounded the crown with separate ridges. Each was checked against the photo at the same bezel diameter, then at Details size.
- **Other views:** checked in the app in Details, Day, Dusk and Night, as an outline and a thumbnail, on the wrist, and on every sample bracelet, NATO, rubber, leather and nylon.
- **Budget:** at `strapLen: 12`, 290 elements and about 36.6 KB, within the 320-element and 50 KB limits.
- **No regressions:** every other catalogue drawing renders byte-for-byte as before (watch, outline, Dusk, Night, wrist and all sample straps), including after the shared bracelet changes.
- **Checks:** `npm run build` passes type checking, all tests and the production build.

## Remaining approximations

- **Type:** TUDOR uses bold Times New Roman and the rest Helvetica or Arial, not Tudor's lettering; the shield is a simple outline.
- **Crystal:** the domed sapphire's edge is a smooth gradient; the photo's is a textured grey.
- **Lugs:** drawn to Tudor's lug-to-lug, so longer than the photo shows them; the case's curvature isn't modelled.
- **Bracelet:** links at one pitch on the shared taper; the T-fit clasp isn't drawn.
- **Glow colour:** a generic green, not confirmed.
