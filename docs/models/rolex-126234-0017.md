# Rolex 126234-0017 "Datejust 36" — illustration notes

Drawn to the [watch illustration standard](../watch-illustration-standard.md), 20 November 2026. The second Rolex in the catalogue, adding White Rolesor (Oystersteel and 18k white gold), the fluted bezel, the Cyclops date and the five-piece Jubilee bracelet.

## Reference and dimensions

- **Photo:** Rolex's front-on m126234-0017 product photo (3200 × 4762 px, RGBA PNG cutout) and its white-background detail crop, supplied for this work. Neither is stored in the repository.
- **Specs:** [m126234-0017 on Rolex](https://www.rolex.com/watches/datejust/m126234-0017) and Rolex's Datejust 36 126234 specification sheets, checked 20 November 2026:
  - **Case:** Oyster, 36 mm, White Rolesor (combination of Oystersteel monobloc middle case, screw-down case back and winding crown with an 18k white-gold bezel).
  - **Bezel:** fluted, 18k white gold.
  - **Crown:** screw-down, Twinlock double waterproofness system.
  - **Crystal:** scratch-resistant sapphire, Cyclops lens over the date.
  - **Water resistance:** 100 metres / 330 feet.
  - **Movement:** calibre 3235, Manufacture Rolex; perpetual, mechanical, self-winding via bidirectional Perpetual rotor; −2/+2 s/day after casing; paramagnetic blue Parachrom hairspring and Paraflex shock absorbers; instantaneous date with rapid setting and stop-seconds; approximately 70 hours. Frequency 28,800 vph ([Monochrome](https://monochrome-watches.com/2019-rolex-datejust-36-steel-rolesor-126234-review-price/), [Fratello](https://www.fratellowatches.com/the-rolex-datejust-with-green-ombre-lacquer-dials-proves-once-more-why-the-36mm-form-reigns-supreme/)), so `beats` is 8.
  - **Bracelet:** Jubilee, five-piece solid links in Oystersteel (ref. 62800), folding Oysterclasp with Easylink 5 mm comfort extension link.
  - **Dial:** bright blue (sunray finish), highly legible Chromalight display with long-lasting blue luminescence.
- **Variant:** bright-blue sunray dial, fluted 18k white-gold bezel, five-piece Jubilee bracelet (`126234-0017`). Preserves this exact configuration rather than smooth-bezel `126200`, Oyster-bracelet variants or 41 mm `126334` proportions (which add five-minute numerals around the minute track).
- **Dimensions Rolex doesn't publish:**
  - **Lug width:** 20 mm, confirmed by the photo's bracelet end link (761.5 px across against the 1370.7 px 36 mm middle case: 20.00 mm) and strap guides ([Delugs](https://delugs.com/blogs/strap-guide/strap-guide-rolex-datejust-126234)).
  - **Lug to lug:** 43.3 mm, from [Fratello](https://www.fratellowatches.com/the-rolex-datejust-with-green-ombre-lacquer-dials-proves-once-more-why-the-36mm-form-reigns-supreme/) ("a 36mm case with a 43.3mm lug-to-lug and an 11.6mm profile"). Some strap guides round this to 43 mm or 44 mm.
  - **Thickness:** 11.6 mm, from [Fratello](https://www.fratellowatches.com/the-rolex-datejust-with-green-ombre-lacquer-dials-proves-once-more-why-the-36mm-form-reigns-supreme/); other reviews quote up to 12 mm depending on whether the Cyclops lens is included.
  - **Clasp width:** not published by Rolex; the Jubilee layout tapers from 20 mm towards 15.5 mm to match the front photo's visible taper.
- **Scale:** one uniform 38.075 px/mm, at which the 20 mm end link spans 761.5 px and the 36 mm middle case at 9 o'clock has half-width 683.5 px (17.95 mm). The photo's lugs measure 43.02 mm end to end because they curve downward away from the lens; the case maps onto the catalogue's 36 × 43.3 mm with `sx = D / 36` and `sy = LL / 43.3`, while the bezel and dial are drawn in photo millimetres scaled by `u = D / 36`.
- **Perspective:** the lug midpoint sits at `y = 2410.0` px, 0.89 mm below the hand pivot (`1599.5, 2376.0`), and the raised bezel rim sits 0.21 mm above the pivot (`y = 2368.0`). Each was measured about its own centre and centred at `(0, 0)` in the drawing.

## Where it lives

- **Selection:** `shape: "rolex-1262340017"` sends the drawing to `rolex1262340017SVG()` in [rolex1262340017.ts](../../src/render/rolex1262340017.ts).
- **Bracelet:** the strap renderer's `jubilee` style with `fiveRow: "dj36"` in [strap.ts](../../src/render/strap.ts): lengthwise-brushed outer satin links (`0.278` of width each) and three rounded `pill`-shaded polished centre rows (`0.148` each) that continue through the end link to the case (`through: true`), staggered half a link between the middle and flanking rows at a 4.1 mm pitch, tapering towards 15.5 mm over 42 mm.

## Live time

The hour, minute and seconds hands are tagged with `handRole()`. The seconds hand sweeps at 8 beats per second (28,800 vph).

## Lume

Rolex states a "Highly legible Chromalight display with long-lasting blue luminescence", and [Monochrome](https://monochrome-watches.com/2019-rolex-datejust-36-steel-rolesor-126234-review-price/) and [Fratello](https://www.fratellowatches.com/hot-take-rolex-datejust-36-reference-126234/) confirm 18k white-gold applied baton hour markers and baton hands filled with Chromalight. Unlike the Submariner, the fluted bezel, 12 o'clock coronet and needle seconds hand carry no lume, so `parts` is `["hands", "indices"]`. Uses the Submariner's Chromalight turquoise glow (`#50D2E0`), `halo: 0.6` and `ambient: 0.5`; marked `likely` because no exact-reference Rolex night image for `126234-0017` was inspected.

## Validation

- **Overlay & reference comparison:** rendered at the photo's 38.075 px/mm and checked at 50% overlay and side by side against both the transparent product cutout and Rolex's white-background detail crop. After inspection in the app UI, the fluted bezel's facet reflection curves were refined so opposing flute slopes never darken simultaneously at 3 o'clock and use softer slate shadows (`#43484e`) with a true scalloped outer rim.
- **Views:** checked in Details, Compare (side by side and outlines), On the wrist (16.5 cm against the Submariner), thumbnails (1.2 px/mm), every strap type (Jubilee, Oyster, leather, NATO, Tropic rubber), Dusk and Night lighting, live-time tags (`beats: 8`), and the generated share preview `dist/share/rolex-126234-0017.jpg`.
- **Budget:** 284 SVG elements and 40,844 UTF-8 bytes at `strapLen: 12` (within the 320-element and 50,000-byte limits).
- **No regressions:** all 189 rendered views across the existing 27 catalogue watches remain byte-for-byte identical. `npm run build` passes type checking, all tests and the production build.

## Remaining approximations

- **Typography:** `ROLEX`, `OYSTER PERPETUAL`, `DATEJUST` and the magnified `28` use standard serif/sans fonts (Tinos and Arimo in share images) rather than Rolex's bespoke dial and date-disc typefaces; the tiny crown-and-coronet micro-details are simplified.
- **Fluted bezel:** six compound tone buckets approximate continuous specular reflections across 152 white-gold facets without per-facet gradients.
- **Dimensions & bracelet taper:** lug-to-lug (43.3 mm) and thickness (11.6 mm) come from Fratello's measurements, and the clasp taper (towards 15.5 mm) is measured from the front photo since Rolex does not publish these figures.
